Applied example — publishers

TIME is already getting paid

In its 103rd year, TIME does not hand its articles to AI for free. It gets paid three different ways, depending on who is asking.

① For the big AI company
License the whole archive
and get paid every year

A multi-year contract with OpenAI (2024). ChatGPT may use 101 years of articles, and TIME receives a set amount each year. Answers carry the source and a link to the original.

② For AI search services
Share the revenue
every time it is cited

When AI search Perplexity answers by citing a TIME article, TIME takes a share of the ad revenue. The more citations, the more revenue.

③ For bots with no contract
"You have to pay
to come in"

Bots without a contract get "pay first (402)". Pay and you pass; don't and you are blocked — running on the Cloudflare network since 2025.
